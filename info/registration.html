The UKICER conference will start on Thursday, 3 September 2026, at 9am (GMT) and finish on Friday, 4 September, at 5pm (GMT). These times are preliminary and might change as soon as the programme is finalised.

Registration includes coffee/tea and lunch on both days of the conference and a 3-course conference dinner at Magdalene College on Thursday evening. We are also offering a printed version of the proceedings and a tote bag with a small selection of sponsored items. These items are free of charge and not funded by the conference fee. You can opt in to receiving these additional items at registration.

Optional pre-conference events: There is the option to participate in a punting excursion on Wednesday afternoon, for which participants will need to pay £10 as a contribution. For those not attending the punting trip, there will be a guided walk of Cambridge offered for free.

Registration Terms and Conditions

  • Participation is not guaranteed until full payment of the registration fee is received.
  • The conference programme may be subject to change.

Accommodation Booking

The closest hotels to the venue are Hyatt Centric Cambridge and Turing Locke Apartments. Both are a 20-minute walk from the conference location and offer parking options.

The venue is also easily reachable by bus from the city centre (bus routes 4, 8, U, X3—see timetable for U bus line) and car (the nearest parking possibility is Madingley Road Park & Ride service, which is a 15-minute walk from the venue).

Participants are of course free to book any accommodation that they prefer. If you need other hotel recommendations, please get in touch with Bonnie Sheppard, one of our organisers.
